# Combos
Each consecutive hit raises the multiplier by 0.1. A miss or expired ring resets the combo.
# Scoring
Base score is 10 times (1 + combo * 0.1). Accuracy and CPS feed the profiler.
# Anti-cheat
Trust score 0-100. Macro-like interval regularity, CPS above 14 and synthetic events reduce it. Below 40 scores are rejected.
# Skins and themes
Cyberpunk Neon, Retro 8-Bit and Synthwave 80s unlock via a 20-combo or 7-day streak.
# Easter eggs
Hit rings clockwise three rounds in a row to reveal the ghost skin.
# Esports strategy
Aim for rhythm, not raw speed. Pre-aim toward the next spawn. Avoid mashing; it hurts accuracy and trust.
