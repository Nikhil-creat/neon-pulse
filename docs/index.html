    Platform

    Everything a ranked arena needs.

    01

    Agentic gameplay director

    Watches CPS and accuracy in real time and retunes spawn speed, ring size and lifetime so every round stays on the edge of your skill.

    02

    Anti-cheat trust score

    Interval deviation, entropy and CPS ceilings give every session a 0 to 100 trust score. Low trust means the score never reaches the board.

    03

    Hybrid RAG engine

    BM25 plus BGE vectors in Qdrant, fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, grounds Click-Bot answers in the official rules and strategy guides.

    04

    Gesture control

    A PyTorch CNN turns webcam taps and fist clenches into clicks, with an OpenCV presence check for ranked play.

    05

    Click-Bot Prime

    Streaming assistant on Groq with Gemini fallback. It calls tools for stats, daily rewards, custom challenges and knowledge search.

    06

    Live leaderboards

    Redis sorted sets power All time, Today, Week and Friends boards, pushed to every player over WebSockets.

    07

    Signed scores

    Every score body is HMAC-SHA256 signed with a per-player key, then re-validated against the mathematical maximum on the server.

    08

    Production telemetry

    Prometheus metrics, JSON logs with correlation IDs, rate limiting, health checks and non-root containers across all services.

    09

    Local-first mode

    Accounts, leaderboards, friends, rewards and achievements all work in the browser with no server. Add a backend later for global boards.

    10

    In-browser AI agent

    Click-Bot calls tools (stats, rewards, challenges, themes, knowledge search) through Groq function calling, or answers with Gemini.

    11

    AI coach and share cards

    Every round gets a personalised coaching report and a score chart, plus a shareable image card.

    12

    Voice and motion control

    Speak commands, hear answers, or tap the air: webcam frame differencing turns hand movement into clicks, fully on-device.

    13

    Command palette

    Press Ctrl+K or / (or tap Commands) to start rounds, switch themes, jump to sections and install the app.

    14

    Game modes

    Classic, Survival (3 lives), Precision (misses cost points), Rush (15 seconds) and a seeded Daily Challenge with a daily twist.

    15

    Power-up rings

    Golden rings pay triple, ice rings slow the spawns, bomb rings punish careless clicks.

    16

    P2P duels

    Challenge a friend with no server: swap two codes over WebRTC and race on identical seeded rounds with live scores.

    17

    A hit heatmap and click-gap histogram show where and how you play. Export or import your whole profile as a backup.

    18

    On-device AI

    Optional WebGPU model (Qwen 0.5B via WebLLM) answers Click-Bot questions with no API key and no network after the first download.

    19

    Agentic AI

    Four agents. One fair game.

    Orchestrated with LangGraph: anti-cheat feeds the director, the profiler and commentator react to the verdict.

    AGENT 1

    Gameplay Director

    Input: CPS, hit precision, reaction variance. Output: spawn velocity, ring radius, lifetime. Runs every second.

    AGENT 2

    Anti-Cheat Guardrail

    Input: click-interval timeline. Output: trust score and flag (macro-like, superhuman CPS, low entropy). Runs on every submitted round.

    AGENT 3

    Match Commentator

    Input: combo streaks, close calls, rank shifts. Output: live commentary lines in the feed.

    AGENT 4

    Behaviour Profiler

    Input: post-match telemetry. Output: a playstyle persona (Speed Demon, Precision Sniper, Tactical Strategist) saved to your profile.

    How it works

    From click to rank in four steps.

    STEP 1

    Play

    Hit the rings in a 30 second round. The Director adapts as you go.

    STEP 2

    Sign

    Your browser signs the score and click timeline with HMAC-SHA256.

    STEP 3

    Verify

    The API checks the signature and maths, then the Anti-Cheat agent scores trust.

    STEP 4

    Rank

    Trusted scores land in Redis boards, your persona updates and the world sees it live.

    FAQ

    Quick answers.

    Is it free to run?

    Yes. Groq and Gemini both have free API tiers, and every service runs in Docker on a laptop or a free-tier VPS.

    Does GitHub Pages run the backend?

    No. Pages hosts only the static site. The Python services run via Docker Compose and the page connects to them using the API URL in the Account box.

    How is cheating prevented?

    Scores are HMAC-signed, checked against the maximum possible for the reported hits, and screened by the Anti-Cheat agent. Trust below 40 rejects the score.

    Where is my data stored?

    Accounts in PostgreSQL, scores and stats in Redis. Without logging in, nothing leaves your browser.

    Can I play offline?

    Yes. Offline mode keeps a local leaderboard in your browser, and Click-Bot answers from the built-in knowledge base.
